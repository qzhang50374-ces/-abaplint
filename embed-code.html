ABAPLINT Playground 嵌入代码

使用以下代码在您的网站中嵌入 ABAPLINT Playground

方法 1: 简单 iframe 嵌入

<iframe 
  src="https://playground.abaplint.org/" 
  width="100%" 
  height="600" 
  style="border: none;"
  title="ABAPLINT Playground">
</iframe>
预览效果

方法 2: 响应式嵌入

<div style="position: relative; width: 100%; padding-bottom: 56.25%; height: 0;">
  <iframe 
    src="https://playground.abaplint.org/" 
    style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%; border: none;"
    title="ABAPLINT Playground">
  </iframe>
</div>

方法 3: 带标题的完整嵌入

<div style="border: 1px solid #ddd; border-radius: 8px; overflow: hidden; max-width: 900px;">
  <div style="background: #252526; color: #fff; padding: 12px 16px; font-family: sans-serif;">
    ABAPLINT Playground
  </div>
  <iframe 
    src="https://playground.abaplint.org/" 
    width="100%" 
    height="500" 
    style="border: none; display: block;"
    title="ABAPLINT Playground">
  </iframe>
</div>
